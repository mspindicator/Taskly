Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Taskly ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website and services.

Information we collect

  • Account information: email address, and authentication details if you sign up via Google or another provider.
  • Usage data: tasks viewed, started, and completed; device and browser information; approximate location (country/region) for fraud prevention and offer eligibility.
  • Payout information: the payment method and account details you provide to receive earnings (e.g. mobile money number, email for PayPal).
  • Identity verification data: if required before a payout, we may collect a government-issued ID and a selfie for verification, processed through a third-party verification provider.

How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To track task completion and calculate rewards owed to you
  • To detect and prevent fraud, including duplicate accounts and automated activity
  • To process payouts to your chosen payment method
  • To send you task and account notifications, which you can control in your settings

Sharing with third parties

We share limited data with our offer providers (to confirm task completion) and payment processors (to send your payouts). We do not sell your personal information to advertisers.

Data retention

We retain account and transaction data for as long as your account is active, and as needed to comply with legal and fraud-prevention obligations after account closure.

Your choices

You can request a copy of your data, ask us to delete your account, or opt out of non-essential notifications at any time by contacting us.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to privacy@taskly.example.
